About

With over 27 years of experience as a DJ, Nick Stene has built a reputation for delivering high-energy performances across a wide range of dance music genres. He has provided direct support for renowned headliners, including Aphrodite (UK), Rob Garza (Thievery Corporation), Steve Duda and Toro Y Moi.

Nick’s versatility shines across genres and venues, from underground events in his hometown of Los Angeles to iconic stages like The Sanctuary art car in Black Rock City and Electric Sheep events at The Battery SF. Known for seamless transitions and his ability to command the dance floor, he has mastered marathon sets, including 11-hour performances, with precision and consistency. Today, Nick is the resident DJ at Coco Maya on Virgin Gorda in the British Virgin Islands.
